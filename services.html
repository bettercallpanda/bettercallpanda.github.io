What I do

Every animal has its own habits and limits. I work in Belgrade and adapt to each one, so you can leave with peace of mind.

In-home pet sitting

I care mainly for cats, as well as dogs, rodents (hamsters, rats) and reptiles, which I’ve also kept myself. Your pet stays in their own home while I keep up meals, play and routines, so there’s no need for a cattery or kennel.

Dog walking

Walks at your dog’s pace and energy level. We explore your neighbourhood and keep up good walking habits.

Vet visits

I take your pet to the clinic as carefully as possible, keep a constant eye on them and keep disturbances to a minimum. At the clinic, I stay with them, follow your instructions and tell you what the vet said. I travel by public transport or taxi, and the owner pays any taxi fare.

How I work

You deal directly with the person caring for your pet. I follow your instructions, never push an animal past what it’s comfortable with, and send photos after every visit.

We agree everything in advance

No middlemen and no unknown sitters. At a free meet-and-greet visit, we go through feeding times, medical needs and your pet’s quirks. After every visit, you get photos and a written report, plus video on request.

Your pet sets the pace

Shelter volunteering and courses in animal psychology and applied ethology taught me to read body language and spot signs of stress. Outgoing or shy, your pet decides how quickly we become friends.
