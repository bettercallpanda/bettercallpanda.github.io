Frequently asked questions

How do you handle my keys?

I usually collect and return them in person, hand to hand. If another way suits you better, we’ll work it out.

What happens if my pet gets ill?

I ask for emergency contacts and your vet’s details in advance. If anything goes wrong, I’ll contact you straight away and book your pet in at your clinic. If you don’t have one, I’ll choose the nearest suitable clinic.

And if my pet goes missing?

I’ll tell you straight away, post in local chats and Facebook groups, and do everything I can to find them. It has never happened on my watch, but I’ve joined searches for lost cats and dogs.

Who pays for vet treatment?

Treatment is paid for by the owner. You can leave a sum with me for emergencies. If you haven’t, I’ll pay myself where I can, send you the receipt and you’ll pay me back. If your pet needs urgent care and I can’t pay upfront, I’ll arrange a deposit with the clinic.

How does payment work?

Cash only for now. You pay a 50% deposit at the meet-and-greet, which secures your dates, and the rest on the day of the first visit.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancel at least 48 hours before the first visit and I’ll refund your full deposit. After that, it’s non-refundable. If I ever have to cancel, you’ll get everything back.

How do we agree the details?

First by phone or message, then in person at the free meet-and-greet. After that, I’ll send you the dates, services, price and cancellation terms, and all you need to do is confirm.

Can I book over the holidays?

Yes, but it’s best to message me early, as dates fill up faster around the holidays.
