Prices for pet sitting, dog walking and vet visits

These are guide prices. Every visit includes one-to-one attention, your pet’s usual routine and a photo update.

How pricing works

We start with a call or messages about what you need. Then I’ll visit free of charge to meet your pet and agree routines and instructions. The final price depends on your area, the number of pets and any medical needs.

Cash only for now: a 50% deposit at the meet-and-greet, with the balance due on the day of the first visit.

All walks are one-to-one. The owner pays any taxi fare.
