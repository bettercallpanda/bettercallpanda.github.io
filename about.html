Hi, I’m Tanya. My friends call me Panda

I look after pets in their own homes in Belgrade. Here’s why you can trust me with both your pet and your keys.

Eight cats and a Belgrade shelter

Animals were part of my life long before Better Call Panda. My eight rescue cats taught me patience and attentiveness. Regular volunteering at the shelter showed me that every animal has its own story and temperament.

My services

What I’ve learned about animal behaviour

I completed the Applied Animal Behaviour course at the School of Applied Ethology under Dr Sofya Baskin, as well as advanced training in Animal and Comparative Psychology at Moscow Zoo (2019). The main thing I took from them: pets need routine, comfort and the right to say no. Your dog may long for an energetic walk while your cat prefers to watch me from a distance. I’ll adapt to them both.
